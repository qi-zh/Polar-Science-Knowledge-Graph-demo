Sample sources

The demo uses two metadata samples and simulated knowledge-source responses.

AMIDER specimen

File: data/amider_specimen.csv
Catalogue number: A00850-0001
Scientific name: Euphausia superba
Record unit: one catalogued biological specimen
Source: https://amider.rois.ac.jp/data/832

ADS penguin survey

File: data/ads_survey.csv
Station: Mame_Island
Observation year: 2020
Campaign: JARE61
Scientific name: Pygoscelis adeliae
Record unit: one station in one annual population survey
Source: https://ads.nipr.ac.jp/dataset/A20210507-018

The ADS sample contains station/year metadata, without population counts.
Its local key, Mame_Island|2020|JARE61, combines the station, year and campaign.

Taxon identities

data/reviewed_identity_mappings.csv supplies the confirmed identities used by
the rules. data/mock_ncbi.json supplies simulated NCBI Taxonomy responses for:

Euphausia superba (TaxID 6819)
https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/Taxonomy/Browser/wwwtax.cgi?id=6819

Pygoscelis adeliae (TaxID 9238)
https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/Taxonomy/Browser/wwwtax.cgi?id=9238

Feeding relation

data/mock_globi.json supplies a simulated GloBI response for:
Pygoscelis adeliae --PREYS_ON--> Euphausia superba
Reference:
https://www.globalbioticinteractions.org/browse/?sourceTaxon=Pygoscelis+adeliae&interactionType=preysOn&targetTaxon=Euphausia+superba&excludeChildTaxa=true

This relation connects the two taxa and supports navigation between their
associated records. Both mock files are manually assembled example responses;
the demo reads them locally rather than calling the external APIs.
